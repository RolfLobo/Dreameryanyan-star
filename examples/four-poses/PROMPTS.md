## 01 · 跳 (mid-hop)

**核心**：她跳起来的那一瞬间被抓拍——不是站着，是有动能。
**头顶符号**：小灯泡（灵感刚被点亮 → 兴奋跳起来）

```text
Generate one standalone 3:4 vertical minimalist newspaper-style editorial illustration — one bold conceptual metaphor, massive white space, restrained warm-ink line work, and unified hand-drawn parallel hatching.

Visual DNA:
Warm off-white cream paper background (#fbf8f0). Around 65% empty paper (mostly the upper and side regions). Confident hand-drawn warm-ink line work. Structural hatching only where volume/shadow makes sense. NO decorative elements, NO sparkles, NO patterns, NO shine.

Recurring IP character (小星妍 · Xingyan):
A small hand-drawn honey-yellow five-pointed star with gently rounded but clearly star-shaped points, drawn with a slight wobble and a subtle tilt of about -8° to the left (mid-air lean). Warm-black wobbly hand-drawn outline (#1a1614), 1-2 px feel with tiny breaks. Two tiny black dot eyes, slightly asymmetric, wide with excitement. A tiny mouth mark is allowed — a small "o" of joy — never a big cartoon grin. Thin black legs caught mid-hop: one leg kicks forward with a lifted foot, the other tucks back with the foot behind. Small foot marks at the end of each leg. No arms drawn in this pose — her whole body IS the jump. 6-10 short parallel diagonal hatching strokes on her lower-right side for volume.

Dreamer signature: directly above her head, tucked close to the crown, a very SMALL handwritten "Dreamer" arcs gently in warm honey ink. Size ~1/9 her body height. Tight small curve, NOT a rainbow.

Status glyph (above the Dreamer arc): a tiny hand-drawn LIGHT BULB in honey-yellow outline only (no fill), floating just above the Dreamer arc — her "idea just sparked" state that made her leap. Size ~1/5 her body height. Simple bulb silhouette with a tiny screw base, NO rays, NO sparkles.

Signature bottom-right: tiny handwritten "妍 ✦" in warm honey ink.

Single visual concept:
Xingyan is caught mid-air in a small joyful hop, entirely lifted off the ground. Her body tilts back slightly with the momentum, one leg kicking forward, the other tucked back. She is in the middle of the empty cream page — the emptiness around her IS the "air" she jumps through. Directly below her, on the ground line, a small trail of 5-6 tiny hatch marks shows where her feet just left off.

Composition (3:4 vertical):
- Upper 45% of canvas: empty cream paper. Only the tiny Dreamer arc + tiny honey light-bulb glyph appear here, hugging the top of her crown.
- Middle band (~35% of canvas): Xingyan mid-hop, centered horizontally, body tilted -8° left, legs bent asymmetrically (forward + back). Small honey-yellow crown perched on her top point.
- Lower 20% of canvas: mostly empty cream, except for a small cluster of 5-6 short parallel hatching strokes on the ground line directly under her — the "she just left from here" trace. Two or three short motion streaks trail behind her body on the lower-left side.

No Chinese labels in this image — the pose speaks alone.

Hatching language (unified):
Warm-black short parallel diagonal strokes at ~45° down-right, three places only: (1) lower-right of Xingyan's body (6-10 strokes), (2) ground trace where she jumped from (5-6 short strokes on the ground line), (3) two or three faint motion streaks trailing her lower-left side (2-3 strokes). NO hatching in the upper or side empty areas.

Color use (strict):
- Paper: warm cream (#fbf8f0).
- Honey-yellow ONLY on Xingyan's body, the small crown on her head, the tiny "Dreamer" arc, and the tiny light-bulb status glyph outline above her head.
- Warm-black ink for: Xingyan's outline, eyes, mouth mark, legs, feet, all hatching, the ground trace, the motion streaks.
- No red, no blue, no orange in this scene.

Anti-slop constraints:
- Xingyan MUST be clearly OFF the ground — visible air gap between her feet and the ground trace below.
- Body tilt is subtle (-8° only), not falling over.
- The star has FIVE gently rounded points, not six, not rounded into a puffy shape.
- NO speed lines, NO motion blur, NO cartoon whoosh — only the tiny hatching trail below and two subtle streaks behind.
- NO gradient, NO plastic shine, NO decorative sparkles, NO extra stars around her.
- Dreamer text tiny, hugs her crown.
- At least 65% of the canvas is empty cream paper.

## 02 · 举东西 (hoisting a ?)

**头顶符号**：小 `?`（追问、走向未知）

A tiny mouth mark is allowed — a short determined horizontal line — never a big cartoon grin. Thin black legs in a wide braced stance, feet planted apart, knees soft. - Red (#E43C31) ONLY for the LARGE hand-drawn "?" she is hoisting overhead — this is the only red in the scene.
- Warm-black ink for: Xingyan's outline, eyes, mouth mark, arms, hand marks, legs, feet, all hatching (including on the red "?"), the ground shadow.
- No blue, no orange in this scene.

Anti-slop constraints:

**核心**：她转身用一只尖角当箭头，明确指向画面外的某个东西——决断感。
**头顶符号**：小放大镜（观察、审视）

Visual DNA:

Recurring IP character (小星妍 · Xingyan):

A tiny mouth mark is allowed — a short determined line — never a big cartoon grin. Thin black legs in a mid-stride pose: one planted, the other slightly forward as if she just stepped into position. ## 04 · 并肩 (side-by-side with AI blob)

**核心**：她和 AI blob 并肩站在同一条地面上，同高、同重、互相挥手——队友时代。
**头顶符号**：小对话气泡（正在协作）

```text
Generate one standalone 3:4 vertical minimalist newspaper-style editorial illustration — one bold conceptual metaphor, massive white space, restrained warm-ink line work, and unified hand-drawn parallel hatching.

Visual DNA:

Recurring IP character (小星妍 · Xingyan):

Warm-black wobbly hand-drawn outline (#1a1614). Two tiny black dot eyes, slightly asymmetric, warm and looking toward the AI creature. A tiny mouth mark is allowed — a barely-there upward arc — never a big cartoon grin. Thin black legs in a relaxed bouncy stance, one slightly forward. Dreamer signature: directly above her head, tucked close to the crown, a very SMALL handwritten "Dreamer" arcs gently in warm honey ink. Size ~1/9 her body height. Tight small curve, NOT a rainbow.

Signature bottom-right: tiny handwritten "妍 ✦" in warm honey ink.

Warm-black short parallel diagonal strokes at ~45° down-right, three places only: (1) lower-right of Xingyan's body (6-10 strokes), (2) shadow side of the AI blob (3-5 strokes, same angle), (3) shared ground shadow line under both characters (8-10 strokes). NO hatching elsewhere.

Color use (strict):
- Paper: warm cream (#fbf8f0).

## 使用说明

1. 打开你惯用的图像模型（Nano Banana / Midjourney v6+ / Sora / Ideogram / 豆包 / 即梦等对文字理解稳的都可以）
2. 从上面 4 段任选一段 `text` 代码块，整段复制粘贴喂进去
3. 出图后按 [references/qa-checklist.md](references/qa-checklist.md) 逐条过一遍
4. 生成好的 PNG 存到 `~/Desktop/Claude skills/` 或 `examples/four-poses/`

## 常见错误 & 修法

| 错误 | 修法 |
|---|---|
| 星星形状变六角/雪花 | 加强 "clearly FIVE-pointed star, not six" |
| Dreamer 变大彩虹弧 | 强调 "size ~1/9 her body height, tight small curve" |
| 蜂蜜黄染到 blob / crown / 背景 | Anti-slop 里明写 "honey-yellow ONLY on Xingyan's body" |
| AI blob 长得像小星妍 | 强调 "clearly ROUNDED blob, visibly NOT star-shaped" |
| 背景变纯白/发灰 | Anti-slop 明写 "warm cream #fbf8f0, NO pure white" |
| 中文字乱（labels 图里） | 4 张 pose 都设计成无 label，避开中文字形不稳定问题 |
